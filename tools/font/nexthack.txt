; NextHack's 8x8 font -- the source of src/font_data.h (python tools/txt2font.py).
;
; "Byteletter" from the ZX Origins collection by DamienG
; (https://damieng.com/zx-origins): "Font based on Byteletter by DamienG".
; Free to use in games with that credit; the one thing its licence forbids is
; redistributing it as a font (re-hosting the files) -- here it is the game's
; own text. Byteletter is textura lowercase under Fraktur capitals; the
; capitals were hard to read in the status line (HP, AC, T:) and in scroll
; labels, so for NextHack (2026-10-12):
;   - every capital but C E G O redrawn on a Roman skeleton with Byteletter's
;     touches: the split stem (#.#) on B D F H K L M N P R U, the hairline
;     tail under a left stem, and the hairline entry of its C/E/G on A I J S T
;     V W X Y Z (Z with a crossbar);
;   - l and 1 were the same flagged stem ("D1v1"): l is a plain stem whose foot
;     curls right, 1 keeps the flag on a flat base; i hung a tail like j;
;   - [ ] were ornaments, * read as a crooked =, and the hairline punctuation
;     (. , : ; < > ! ? ' - /) vanished on the 128K: all redrawn in 2-pixel
;     strokes and 2x2 dots.
; Each glyph: '@ <hex code> <char>', then 8 rows of # (ink) and . (paper).
